L6 · Composition Plan

Color theme: turquoise, warm sandstone, and muted clay. This is an editorial design direction inspired by turquoise and the regional landscape, not a claim that one palette represents all Indigenous peoples or all Diné artistic traditions.

Project: More Than Words: Keeping the Diné Language Alive. The article remains readable in normal source order. Grid controls the page regions; positioning is reserved for the bounded relationships below.

Element Content purpose Normal-flow baseline Chosen technique and why Containing block / stacking context Fallback or disengagement Required test
.issue-badge Displays a short topic label without interrupting the headline and introduction. The badge follows the lede in source order and remains part of the header content. position: absolute with logical insets; it is anchored to the header rather than the viewport. .feature-header has position: relative and establishes the containing block. The badge uses --layer-raised. At widths up to 24rem, it moves to the upper-left and the header reserves extra top padding. With CSS disabled, it returns to normal inline flow. 320px, long-title growth, 200% zoom, keyboard focus order, and CSS-disabled reading order.
.story-guide Provides in-page navigation to the story sections. The aside appears before the article in the DOM and occupies the first grid cell. position: sticky with inset-block-start keeps navigation available during long-page reading without removing it from flow. The sticky item is in the first row of the main grid; that row is shared with the article and bounds the sticky travel. It uses --layer-sticky. Sticky is enabled only at 52rem and wider. At narrower widths and with CSS disabled, navigation remains a normal-flow block. Scroll from page top through article and to the end of the article/grid row; confirm it stops at the containing-block boundary and links activate.
.pull-quote Emphasizes an editorial summary without presenting it as a quotation from a real speaker. The blockquote follows the feature figure in source order and precedes the article paragraphs. At desktop widths, a negative block-start margin creates a restrained overlap into the figure's reserved empty lower padding. The opaque quote background keeps the figure caption visible. Relative positioning and --layer-raised; it overlaps only the reserved padding area of the preceding .feature-figure, not text or controls. Overlap is disabled below 52rem; the quote returns to a positive normal-flow margin. Without CSS, it is an ordinary blockquote. 768px and 1280px, doubled paragraph content, 200% zoom, and pointer/keyboard checks for nearby links.
.feature-figure and its image Provides an introductory visual while its caption explains that the image is an abstract starter illustration. The figure and caption are in normal article flow. Rounded corners provide a visual frame. A polygon clip-path is a decorative crop enhancement only. The figure is the local visual frame. No arbitrary high layer is applied. The clip path affects only the image, not the caption or story text. Rounded corners are nonessential decoration. If clip-path is unsupported or disabled, the image displays as a complete rectangle. Disable clip-path in DevTools and confirm the complete image and caption remain visible; inspect at 320px, 768px, and 1280px.
.shape-portrait Adds a secondary abstract illustration beside the section about intergenerational learning. The image is a block in the article flow, before the paragraphs that describe language learning. At desktop widths, float: inline-start, shape-outside: circle(50%), and shape-margin create an editorial text wrap. The float participates in the containing article section's flow; display: flow-root on each article section contains the float. Float and shape wrapping are applied only inside @supports and at 52rem and wider. Otherwise the image is centered in normal flow and the prose stays full width. Disable shape-outside; verify text returns to full-width flow. Add doubled paragraph text and confirm the float is passed and later text returns to full measure.

Layer scale

  • --layer-base: 0 — baseline layer.
  • --layer-raised: 10 — anchored badge and pull quote.
  • --layer-sticky: 20 — sticky story navigation.
  • --layer-overlay: 30 — reserved token; no overlay is currently required.

Layer values are tokens rather than one-off numbers. No decorative element intercepts pointer input. The page avoids clipped essential content, fixed-height prose boxes, and arbitrary positional columns.
